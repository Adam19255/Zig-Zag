## 🎮 Game Overview
- **Genre**: Hyper-casual, endless runner/reflex game  
- **Platform**: Windows  
- **Team**: Adam Shay, Evyatar Ben Chayun, Bar Cohen  

Zig-Zag is a fast-paced, hyper-casual arcade game where players guide a rolling ball along a winding, zigzagging path. With simple one-click controls, the challenge lies in timing direction changes to avoid falling off the edge. The goal is to survive as long as possible while collecting gems along the way. Easy to learn but hard to master, Zig-Zag delivers quick, addictive gameplay perfect for short play sessions.

## 🕹️ How to Play

### 1. Starting the Game
- Launch the game from your Unity project.
- On the main menu, click **Play**.
- Press the **Right Mouse Button** or **Space Bar** to begin.

### 2. Gameplay Basics
- The ball rolls automatically along a zigzag path.
- Click to change direction.
- Avoid falling off the edge.
- Earn points with every successful turn.
- Collect gems to boost your score.

### 3. Levels / Stages
- The game is endless — difficulty increases as you progress.
- The path changes color to indicate progression.

---

## 🎮 Controls
- **Change Direction**: Right Mouse Button or Space Bar  
- **Pause / Resume**: Esc key

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of Zig-Zag is to guide the player along an endless, zigzagging path without falling off, using quick reflexes and precise timing.

### Primary Goal
- Survive as long as possible by staying on the path.
- Successfully change direction at the right moments to avoid falling.
- The longer you stay on the path, the higher your score.

### Secondary Goals
- Collect gems to increase your score.
- Unlock and equip cosmetic skins using collected gems.

---

## ⚙️ Game Mechanics & Features

### 1. Inventory System
- Access the **Shop Menu** from the Main Menu.
- Purchase and equip ball skins with gems.
- Skins are purely cosmetic and let you personalize your in-game experience.

### 2. Score & Progress Tracking
- Your score increases by **+1 point** with every successful click (direction change).
- Gems randomly appear along the path – collecting one gives you **+1 point**.
- Your score is visible during gameplay and used to track personal bests.
- Try to beat your personal best each run!

### 3. Levels & Environment
- The game world evolves as you play. Every 50 points, the tile color changes, giving a visual sense of progression.
- The game becomes more visually dynamic the longer you survive, creating an engaging challenge loop.
